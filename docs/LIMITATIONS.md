# Limitations

## Verification boundary

This repository contains statically reviewed construction source. Dependencies, TypeScript/lint checks, tests, builds, demos, generators, evaluation, provider calls, Word/LibreOffice compatibility, migrations, RLS, auth and deployment have not executed. Syntax parsing and manual source tracing do not establish runtime correctness or security.

The library lock remains UNLOCKED. Paid-model prices remain absent. These deliberately prevent successful agreement export and paid dispatch until activation checks and reviewed configuration changes.

## Product boundaries

- One illustrative tenancy-style agreement, with invented jurisdiction values. No real-law review or jurisdiction claim.
- Fifteen bounded fields, a fixed self-authored clause library, plain paragraph DOCX and Markdown. No clause editor, PDF, signing, email sending, OCR, upload or collaboration.
- Narrow role/cue grammar and restrictive free-text punctuation/script rules trade convenience for bounded document values. Abbreviations or unsupported wording can require a manual answer. Impossible dates can appear as missing rather than carrying a detailed invalid-date reason.
- Optional guarantor/break terms require a blocking confirmation; partial guarantees and pet exceptions require resolution and may leave unsupported residuals.
- Correct grounding proves a value came from the submitted intake; it does not establish the truth of the intake.
- Demo data is memory-only. JSON exports and provenance include normalized source material and should be handled as private documents.
- Live ownership and recovery are source integrations with pending two-user/session regression checks. The account-reset repair has not been exercised in a browser.
- The independent DOCX gate accepts a deliberately narrow vocabulary. Compatibility, malformed-archive handling, decompression bounds and performance require the recorded runtime checks.
- Optional Noul screening is omitted under the plan's allowed cut order. Deterministic validation and the independent gate remain in source.

## Evaluation limits

The twenty authored hand traps and generator proposal are synthetic. Related phrasing variants can be easier than real messages; even thirty held-out scenarios would provide limited statistical power. No accuracy, cost, latency, security-pass or superiority number has been measured.

Jev excludes names/addresses and computed end dates from its eligible comparison and remains optional. Promotion requires complete matched eligible held-out rows, wrong-fill no higher than both baselines and a coverage/accuracy/cost benefit. Source/mock conformance is reported separately from model quality.

Local budget locks are for one machine, not distributed OneDrive coordination. Interrupted attempts, stale locks, corrupt journals and ambiguous I/O require manual audit; unknown exposure is retained. Do not remove budget files to manufacture a retry. Model IDs, API contracts, token/billing bounds and prices require current verification before paid activation.

The strict free-draft paragraph diagnostic also counts unmatched boilerplate. Reports separately classify exact known boilerplate and unrecognized legal content; heading prefixes do not exempt arbitrary hostile text. Synthetic and small-sample results would not certify a real legal agreement.
